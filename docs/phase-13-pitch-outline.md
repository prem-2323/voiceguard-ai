# VoiceGuard AI pitch deck

The editable eight-slide deck is saved in the user's Spaces drive as **VoiceGuard AI — Hackathon Pitch**.

1. **VoiceGuard AI** — Multilingual, voice-first worker safety workflow; Tamil, English, and Hindi; fictional demo.
2. **The worker safety handoff** — Understand, check, report, and follow up in one workflow.
3. **One connected worker journey** — Sign in, choose an assigned active permit and language, use approved content, confirm a report, and read the saved response.
4. **Hazard reports require confirmation** — Speech or text creates a draft; the worker reviews and confirms; an idempotency key supports safe retry.
5. **Supervisor review and acknowledgement** — Assignment-scoped hazard, assessment-review, and simulated SOS queues with audit history and worker updates.
6. **Safety controls set clear limits** — Approved-content gate, explicit answer criteria, escalation to human review, and clear demo boundaries.
7. **A small stack, no paid AI required** — React/Vite, FastAPI, SQLAlchemy/SQLite, browser speech, optional local Faster-Whisper, and Docker Compose.
8. **Demo readiness** — Walkthrough path, verified build/test checks, missing Docker image-build verification, and the reviewer approval gate for safety content.

The deck avoids unverified industrial incident statistics and distinguishes simulated capabilities from operational systems.
